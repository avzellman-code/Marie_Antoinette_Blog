Adieu, Former Subjects!
16 octobre 1793
They allowed me one final post from prison. before they put my head through the chopper. The "guillotine", they call it. So that's what that one troll who kept commenting meant by their username.

My hair's like, really short. Like, a dude's. I guess it's only appropriate the rest of my head go along with it. To be honest, it's been fun being your queen, quite exquisite, if I do say so myself. All of the fun extravagant stuff is what any girl would wish for, to be honest. Well, except for the revolution stuff.

May this new government not screw you all over. And hopefully, there's plenty of bread to go around, none of that cake crap. Like I said, I NEVER said "Let them eat cake"! That's so ridiculous, even for me.

Anyway, I'm... I'm scared, to be honest. Wouldn't you be if you were seconds away from getting your head chopped off?

Not to be so morbid... but I have to go now. Those footsteps are coming for ME.

Adieu.

— Marie

Ongoing Security and Structure Measures
8 octobre 1791
Recent months have involved a necessary continuation of security measures within the Tuileries, established to ensure the orderly function of residence and court life under current conditions.

These measures include structured supervision of movement within designated areas of the household, as well as coordinated oversight of external access points to maintain consistency and safety across all levels of operation.

It is standard in periods of national transition for adjustments of this nature to be implemented in order to preserve continuity of governance and residence function.

All members of the household continue to operate within these arrangements in accordance with established procedure.

Recent events in Paris have required additional attention from governing bodies to ensure that order is maintained in public spaces. Such developments are part of the broader context in which all institutional responsibilities are currently exercised.

Communication continues through formal channels, and all necessary correspondence is being handled with appropriate oversight and review.

Stability remains the shared objective of all parties involved in current administration.
