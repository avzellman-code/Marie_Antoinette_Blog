An Afternoon at the Petit Trianon
3 septembre 1788
Today was spent primarily at the Petit Trianon, which continues to provide a most agreeable change of atmosphere from the greater pace and formality of Versailles proper.

A considerable amount of attention has recently been given to the maintenance and arrangement of the grounds, and the results are quite noticeable. The pathways are especially pleasant at this time of year, and several adjustments to the gardens have improved movement throughout the estate considerably.

The afternoon itself proceeded very smoothly despite a somewhat unusual level of activity and noise reported farther beyond the outer grounds earlier in the day. Fortunately, none of this interfered with the schedule already arranged for the household.

Refreshments were served outdoors for part of the afternoon, which I found particularly enjoyable given the weather.

There is something deeply reassuring about spaces that remain carefully maintained regardless of changing atmosphere elsewhere.

— Marie
