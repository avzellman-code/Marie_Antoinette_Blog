About Me
Welcome, adored readers, to the personal musings of Marie Antoinette, Queen of France and, quite frankly, a woman of exceptional taste.

Here, I shall be sharing glimpses into life at court—fashion, festivities, reflections, and the occasional inconvenience (which I endure with remarkable grace). It has come to my attention that many are curious about how one manages such a life of refinement, and I am simply delighted to provide guidance.

If there are whispers beyond the palace gates, I assure you they are being handled. In the meantime, there is silk to wear, cakes to admire, and a nation to lightly oversee.

Do try to keep up.

— Marie
