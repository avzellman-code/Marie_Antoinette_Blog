A New Chapter of Royal Presence
22 mai 1774
The early days of this new formal era have been marked by a steady continuation of court life, with particular emphasis on public presence and ceremonial visibility.

There is a clear and deliberate effort within Versailles to ensure that transitions of role are expressed with clarity, dignity, and appropriate grandeur. I have been present for several audiences and formal appearances that reflect this intention.

The court has, as always, demonstrated a remarkable ability to maintain coherence across a wide range of events and obligations. It is impressive to observe how seamlessly each part contributes to the overall function of the institution.

I have been advised that continued participation in these activities will remain an important aspect of my position, particularly in relation to public representation and courtly engagement.

It is an honour to take part in this ongoing expression of royal continuity.

— Marie
