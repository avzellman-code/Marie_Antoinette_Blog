The Future of the Crown Continues
11 février 1785
Much attention has recently been directed toward the continued growth and expansion of the royal household.

The presence of children within the court brings a renewed sense of continuity to Versailles, reinforcing the enduring structure and future of the Crown itself. Their development, education, and presentation remain subjects of considerable care and organization within the household.

Preparations are also underway for the arrival of my third child, and the atmosphere within the residence has reflected an appropriate sense of anticipation surrounding this continuation of the royal line.

It is reassuring to witness how effectively Versailles accommodates both tradition and expansion while maintaining the same standards of refinement expected of the institution.

Despite increasingly excessive commentary from certain corners of public discourse, the responsibilities and functions of the court continue uninterrupted and with full attention to propriety.

The future, as ever, remains carefully maintained.

— Marie
