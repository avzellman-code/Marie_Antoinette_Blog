Hello from prison i guess
01 janvier 1793
This is my big "6 month blog post" the prison lets me do I guess. Yep, I'm in prison. It's miserable. They don't even call me by my name, they call me "Widow Capet". It's humiliating. Eh, better than "Baguette Bitch", which they call this one girl who stole a loaf of bread. Huh. Imagine. Life in prison. For a loaf of bread.

I'm not even gonna pretend to put on the whole "let them eat cake" act because what's the point. Screw it. My husband's dead, make all the incest jokes you want. Eat all the goddamn cake you want. I never even said that stupid phrase in the first place. Screw all of you.


— Marie
