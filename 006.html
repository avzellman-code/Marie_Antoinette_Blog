The Beginning of a New Formal Era
15 mai 1774
It has been formally acknowledged that I now serve in the role of Queen Consort of France.

This transition marks an important continuation of established court tradition, and I am informed that it reflects the enduring stability and dignity of the Crown.

The court has, as expected, responded with appropriate ceremony and refinement. The atmosphere today has been especially composed, with a noticeable emphasis on continuity, order, and presentation befitting the occasion.

I am told this role carries both visibility and responsibility within the broader structure of Versailles, and I will, of course, continue to participate in matters of court life with the expected level of decorum and attentiveness.

It is an honour to be situated within such a long-standing institution of culture and excellence.

— Marie
