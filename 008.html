The early rhythm of this formal era continues to develop with consistency and refinement.

Recent attention within broader conversation has naturally increased, as is expected during any period of heightened visibility. It is common for moments of transition to generate interpretation from many different perspectives, not all of which reflect the structured reality of court life.

It is both natural and understandable that narratives form around institutions of this scale. What remains important is the accuracy and continuity of what is actually observed within the court itself.

I remain committed to participating fully in the responsibilities of this role as they are presented and enacted.
