A Morning of Formal Engagements
14 avril 1775
But I digress. This evening’s gathering at Versailles was attended with remarkable enthusiasm and visibility.

The halls remained active well into the later hours, with guests arriving in exceptional numbers throughout the course of the event. It is always encouraging to observe such continued public interest in court life and ceremonial culture.

Music, refreshments, and conversation were all maintained to the expected standard despite increasing levels of activity beyond the palace grounds earlier in the day. I am informed that certain disruptions occurred within Paris, though these appear to have had no meaningful effect on the evening’s proceedings.

The court continues to demonstrate admirable consistency in preserving structure and refinement regardless of external atmosphere.

Particular attention was also given to presentation and arrangement this evening, and I believe the results reflected very favourably on the household as a whole.

It is reassuring to see excellence maintained so reliably during periods of heightened public energy.

— Marie
